# Changelog

All notable changes to EasyFind: Notes Extension are documented in this file.

---

## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-23

### Added
- **EasyFind's note switches on this page**: where notes find you, notes on tooltips, names in chat and player menus, the Ctrl+right-click hint and prices sit under the window's own settings, so every notes setting is in one place (with EasyFind's next update)

### Fixed
- **Settings text**: the note color and situation chip rows use the same text size as the rest of the page

### Changed
- Marked up to date for WoW 12.1.7, so the game no longer lists it as out of date there

---

## [1.0.1] - 2026-11-22

### Fixed
- **Linked notes**: a note linked in chat names its owner in full in a session where the game gives no realm name, so a click on the link still asks the right player for it (with EasyFind 5.6.0)

---

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-03

### Added
- **Notes as their own extension**: EasyFind's Notes, now an addon of its own; every note, checklist and setting you had carries over
- **Notes that know where they apply**: for places, activities, classes, targets and players, in tooltips, on names in chat, or opening by themselves
- **Checklists the game ticks**: item counts, quests, reputations, gold, levels, skills, lockouts and cooldowns, cleared on the reset you pick
- **Shopping lists**: items typed by name that tick once your bags hold enough, an Add item line under the list, prices and a total with the Auctionator extension, and Ctrl+click on a line to search it at the auction house
- **Windows**: each note in its own window, notes that open with the game's windows, reminders, tags, a trash with undo, and notes shared as links
