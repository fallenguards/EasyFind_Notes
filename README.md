# EasyFind: Notes Extension

Notes that know where they apply, checklists the game ticks off for you, and shopping lists that count what is in your bags, all found from EasyFind's search bar.

## Requirements

- EasyFind 5.0.0 or later

Retail, WoW Forever, Classic Era, TBC Anniversary and Mists Classic.

## Features

- Notes for places, activities, classes, targets and players: they show in tooltips and on names in chat, or open by themselves where they apply.
- Checklists the game ticks: a line ticks itself for an item count, a quest, a reputation, gold, a level, a skill, a lockout or a cooldown, and clears on the reset you pick.
- Shopping lists: the names you type are items, each line ticks once your bags hold enough, and with the Auctionator extension each line shows what is left to buy at its price and the list its total.
- At the auction house, Ctrl+click a shopping list line to search it.
- Notes for the game's windows: one tagged Auction House, Merchant or Bank opens with that window.
- Each note in its own window, as many as you like; reminders, tags in any color, a trash with undo, and notes shared as a link in chat.
- Ctrl+Right-click on any item or spell for its note.

## How to use it

Type a note's name in EasyFind's search bar, or `@notes` to list them all, and click one to open it. Open Notes from EasyFind's extensions menu or with `/notes`, and right-click the + button to start a checklist or a shopping list. Your notes from EasyFind's built-in Notes carry over. Its settings are in EasyFind's options, under Extensions > Notes.
